# mcring v1 — PCBA files: what `mcring_v1_cpl.csv` and `mcring_v1_bom_jlc.csv` assume

Generated by `hardware/pcb/export_cpl.py` from `floorplan.py` (positions) and `hardware/electrical/mcring_v1.py` (values, MPNs, LCSC numbers). How to use them: `docs/10-ordering-assembled-boards.md`.

## Coordinate frame and rotation

* Origin = the board's front-left corner seen from the top; X along the finger (0 = camera/FPC edge, 26.5 = antenna edge), Y across it (0 = switch edge, 22.8 = connector-notch edge); mm. Both layers in the top view (bottom parts are *not* mirrored).
* Before exporting Gerbers from the layout tool, put its drill/place origin on that corner with the same axes, or regenerate the CPL from the tool (KiCad: File → Fabrication Outputs → Component Placement, then rename Ref/PosX/PosY/Rot/Side to Designator/Mid X/Mid Y/Rotation/Layer).
* Rotation: 0 for every part except 0402s placed along y (90). The pin-1 orientation of the ICs, the module, FPC1 and the four Pico-EZmate headers is set at layout (each header's wire-exit end must face the right board edge / its notch, D1's DIN toward R7, D3's cathode toward U7, the mic's port over the board hole). JLCPCB re-orients polarised parts to their library footprint at DFM review — confirm every polarised part on their placement preview before approving.

## Counts

* 73 placed parts (10 top, 63 bottom), 31 BOM lines, ≈ 289 solder joints (pads of the placed parts, incl. the module's 65).
* BOM lines with an LCSC number: 14; consigned (not stocked by LCSC): 3; no C-number recorded: 14 (generic passives the JLCPCB BOM tool matches on value + package).

## Not in the CPL / BOM

### Copper features (no component)

* BZ1: two bare 1.2 mm pads (optional piezo, DNP)
* J2: two bare 1.2 mm pads (aux bench experiment)
* TPAD1: 3 x 4 mm copper landing (touch)
* TP1–TP8: 1.0 mm test pads on the top side

### Do-not-populate

* SW2 B3U-1000P (B3U-1000P): optional IO0 boot button (top-actuated), DNP
* U4 (XC6206P122MR, 1.2 V DVDD LDO) is populated in these files; make it DNP in the BOM upload if the purchased OV2640 head regulates DVDD itself (`hardware/electrical/README.md` §1 item 3)
* The optional speaker group U10/U11/C25/C26/R20–R24 is populated here; the J5 header stays either way

### Off-board (owner-supplied, plug in after delivery)

* ANT1 2.4 GHz flex antenna, MHF1 (146153-0050): 2.4 GHz flex dipole 34.9 x 9 x 0.1 mm, 50 mm MHF1 (u.FL) pigtail -> U1 u.FL (mated plug 2.9 mm tall = board_params top_max_height). Route down the back wall / along the band, >= 5 mm from cell and LRA; size vs the band is a fit check
* BT1 301012 LiPo 30 mAh (301012 (30 mAh, with PCM)): 3 x 10 x 12 mm LiPo cell with PCM, 1C max charge; leads terminated in the W1 Pico-EZmate plug (cable shop / solder-seal sleeve, docs/10) and plugged into J1 — no solder on the board
* W1 Pico-EZmate 2-ckt pigtail (36920-0201 (cut in half)): BAT: 28 AWG pre-crimped pigtail, plug into J1; bare end to the cell's two leads
* W2 Pico-EZmate 2-ckt pigtail (36920-0201 (cut in half)): CHG: 28 AWG pre-crimped pigtail, plug into J3; bare ends to CP1/CP2 through the band channels
* CP1 charging contact 3x2 mm ENIG (no MPN): inner-band pogo contact, 3 x 2 mm ENIG, flush, >= 3 mm from the other pad; +5 V from the pogo cradle; the W2 pigtail's bare end is bonded to it with silver conductive pen (<= 25 mA path) or soldered by the cable shop (docs/10)
* CP2 charging contact 3x2 mm ENIG (no MPN): inner-band pogo contact, 3 x 2 mm ENIG, flush, >= 3 mm from the other pad; GND return; the W2 pigtail's bare end is bonded to it with silver conductive pen (<= 25 mA path) or soldered by the cable shop (docs/10)
* M1 LRA0825BC-0167F (LRA0825BC-0167F): 8 x 2.5 mm coin LRA, 240 Hz, 1.25 Vrms max (set RATED_V/OD_CLAMP for 1.25 Vrms @ 240 Hz, docs/07-build-guide.md §1.8); off-board in the floor pocket, leads -> W3 plug -> J4
* W3 Pico-EZmate 2-ckt pigtail (36920-0201 (cut in half)): LRA: 28 AWG pre-crimped pigtail, plug into J4; bare end to the LRA's two leads
* PZ1 PKMCS0909E4000-R1 (PKMCS0909E4000-R1): 4 kHz piezo sounder 9 x 9 x 1.9 mm (LTB) — optional; fit only if the enclosure gets a 9 x 9 x 2 pocket (none in the current CAD); any 2-wire piezo element also works
* LS1 AS01008MR-2-R (AS01008MR-2-R): 10 mm x 2.9 mm (max) 8 ohm micro speaker, 300 mW rated / 500 mW max, 86 dB, top port, NdFeB magnet, solder pads; in the enclosure's speaker pocket (board_params.json speaker_mm), its leads -> W4 plug -> J5; polarity irrelevant (single driver)
