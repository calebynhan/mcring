Touch Ring Build Sheet

Nothing sits on top. Like the Even R1 and Oura, everything lives inside an 8.4 × 2.9 mm band: one flex circuit wrapped around the inside, a curved 15–20 mAh cell, a resin outer wall with the touch slider just under it, and clear epoxy for the inner surface. The circled numbers in the drawings are the item numbers in the parts list.

Build order

  1. Write the firmware on the bench board (#22) while the flex is being made: gestures, BLE HID, mcring events, and a bootloader that takes updates over Bluetooth.
  2. Draw the flex in KiCad from the flat layout above and order 5 assembled boards from JLCPCB.
  3. Bring each flex up while it is still flat: 3.7 V on the battery pads, flash over the SWD pads, check touch through a 0.6 mm sheet of plastic, and check Bluetooth range.
  4. Solder the curved cell to the tab pads, quickly, on the tabs only. Insulate with Kapton.
  5. Solder a 2.2 mm gold pin upright on each contact pad.
  6. Curve the flex into the printed shell, slider side against the outer wall, and tack it with a drop of epoxy.
  7. Pour clear epoxy in two passes and let the bubbles rise. Once cured, sand the inside smooth and the contact pins flush.
  8. Charge it on the dock. From here on, firmware updates go over Bluetooth only.
  • After potting there is no wire access. Prove an update over Bluetooth works before step 7.
  • Keep all copper, the cell and the contacts away from the module's antenna end, and check range before potting.
  • The layout is drawn for size 10. Below size 8 the flex is too long: lay it out again with a shorter cell.
  • The outer wall must be plastic or ceramic. A zirconia shell made by the UIUC furnace route drops into the same design.
  • Use only a cell with its protection board, and never heat the cell body.
