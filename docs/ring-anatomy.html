How to read this

Electronics are drawn as their bounding blocks, the same simplified solids the fit check uses. The shell, lid and switch key are the real print geometry. Source: hardware/cad/mcring_stage1.py reading hardware/pcb/board_params.json. Print files: hardware/cad/out/*.stl. Nothing here has been printed or test-fitted with real parts yet.
Stage 1 is the wearable prototype: bigger than a commercial ring so an ESP32-S3 board, a 30 mAh cell and a speaker fit. Stage 2 targets an Oura-size 8 mm band around an nRF52840 and a curved 16–24 mAh cell; it has no CAD model yet.
